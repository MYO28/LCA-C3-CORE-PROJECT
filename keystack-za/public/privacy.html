Privacy

Keystack ZA respects customer privacy and only collects information needed to provide accounts, carts, orders and customer support.

001

Information we collect

Account details such as name and email address, shipping information provided during checkout, cart contents and order history.

002

How information is used

Information is used to manage accounts, process orders, provide support, communicate order updates and improve the shopping experience.

003

Payment information

Payment details are handled through the checkout payment provider. Keystack ZA does not store full card numbers in the store database.

004

Customer choices

Customers can request access to their account information and ask for corrections where appropriate. Security and retention procedures are reviewed as the business grows.
