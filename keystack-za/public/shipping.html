Shipping information

Keystack ZA delivers keyboard and desk accessories to customers across South Africa, with delivery details shown during checkout.

001

Delivery areas

Orders are prepared for delivery within South Africa. Delivery availability can vary by address and courier coverage.

002

Dispatch

Orders are checked, packed and prepared for courier collection after payment has been confirmed.

003

Tracking

Customers can view their order progress from their account. A tracking number can be added once an order has been dispatched.

004

Delivery support

If an order is delayed or arrives with an issue, customers can contact support with their order reference so the delivery can be investigated.
