Payment details

Keystack ZA supports straightforward checkout in South African Rand (ZAR) with secure payment options suited to local customers.

001

Payment methods

The checkout supports card and instant-EFT style payment options, with the payment gateway selected before the order is confirmed.

002

ZAR pricing

All catalogue prices and order totals are displayed in South African Rand so customers can review the full purchase amount before payment.

003

Order confirmation

After a successful payment step, the customer receives an order reference and can view the order status from their account.

004

Secure handling

Keystack ZA is designed so sensitive payment information is handled by the payment provider rather than stored directly in the store database.
