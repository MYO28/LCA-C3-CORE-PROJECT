# Product pricing notes

Keystack ZA is a student/demo store, so these are **realistic benchmark prices**, not live quotations or claims that Keystack ZA sells the named products at these prices.

The catalogue was priced using current South African retail listings for comparable products. For example, Wootware currently lists custom/mechanical keyboards ranging from budget models below R1,000 to premium Keychron and other models above R2,000, while The HOB lists Keychron custom keyboards, PBT keycap sets, switches and coiled cables in similar ranges. Wootware lists a Keychron K4 HE at about R3,021 and a Keychron Q1 HE at about R4,141, while The HOB lists Keychron K7 Max at about R2,209, PBT keycap sets around R539–R659, and Keychron coiled cables around R309.

For the Premium Magnetic Switch 60% Keyboard, The manufacturer reference lists the pre-assembled aluminium version at US$239.99. A South African price of R3,999 with a promotional display price of R2,999 is therefore used as a reasonable imported-premium placeholder, rather than being presented as an official Wooting sale.

The exact Keystack ZA values are rounded student-project retail prices intended to demonstrate product pricing, discounts and checkout calculations.
